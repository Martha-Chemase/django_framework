When will my order ship?+

Most orders leave our studio in 1–3 business days. We'll send an email when your parcel is on its way.

How much does shipping cost?+

Standard shipping is complimentary on orders over $150. Shipping for other orders is shown at checkout.

Can I return or exchange a piece?+

Unworn pieces can be returned within 30 days of delivery. Email us with your order number and we'll help with next steps.

How should I care for my Forma pieces?+

Check the care label on each piece for its specific instructions. When in doubt, gentle care and airing between wears can help your favourites last.

How do I get in touch?+

Write to hello@formastudio.com. A real person from our team will be in touch within two business days.
